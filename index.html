ISO 27001

The functional layer. Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, and Govern — a common language for talking about maturity with your board, insurer, and regulators.

The technical baseline. Eight concrete mitigation strategies — patching, application control, MFA, restricted admin privileges — scored on maturity levels 0 through 3.

Make it uncrackable, and easy to remember

  • Use a passphrase. Four or more random words are far stronger, and easier to recall, than a short complex password.
  • Length beats complexity. Every extra character multiplies the effort to crack it.
  • Never reuse passwords. One leak should not unlock everything. A password manager makes this painless.
  • Turn on MFA. Even a stolen password is far less useful when multi-factor authentication is in the way.

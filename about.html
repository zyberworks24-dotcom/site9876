ABOUT ZYBERWORKS

Security should be
designed in, not bolted on.

We are an IT services and security consultancy for organisations that cannot afford to get security wrong. We bring enterprise grade tooling and process, and pair it with plain speaking advice and a team that stays accountable for the outcome.

Most breaches do not start with a genius exploit. They start with a gap nobody mapped. Our job is to find those gaps before an attacker does, close them in the right order, and keep them closed as your organisation grows and changes.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Four principles
we do not compromise on.

Pragmatism

We fix the highest risk first with what you have, rather than selling a rebuild you do not need.

Independence

Accountability

One team that owns the result from first assessment through to ongoing assurance.

Clarity

No jargon walls. You always know what we found, what it means, and what happens next.

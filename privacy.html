Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

This policy explains what information Zyberworks collects through this website and how we use it. We keep it deliberately short, because this website collects very little.

Information we collect

This website does not use tracking cookies or third party analytics, and it does not store your details on our servers. The only information we handle is what you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, email address, optional company name, the service you are interested in, and your message.

The contact form composes that information into an email in your own mail application. Nothing is sent until you review it and press send. Your enquiry is delivered to enquiry@zyberworks.com.au.

How we use it

We use the information you send only to respond to your enquiry and to provide the services you ask about. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties except where it is necessary to deliver a service you have engaged us for.

Third party links

This site links to our technology partners and to external resources. Once you follow a link to another website, that site's own privacy practices apply. We encourage you to read them.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Email enquiry@zyberworks.com.au and we will respond promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
